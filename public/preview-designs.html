Weekend Quest UI Direction

Three bespoke design concepts to break out of the generic AI template and build an authentic, memorable product.

Option A · Partiful / Nightclub Pass

The Tactile Ticket Board

High contrast dark-slate ground with tangible cream ticket-stub cards. Directly schedules 72 hours across Friday, Saturday, and Sunday columns with perforated slips, venue chips, and an integrated group voting & chat rail.

  • Friday / Saturday / Sunday Columns
  • Physical Perforated Ticket Stubs
  • Live Group Polls & Chat Sidecar
  • Tear-off notch & venue chips
Option B · Monocle / Kinfolk

The Editorial Weekend Guide

Clean warm daylight mode, editorial magazine typography, and structured graphic columns. Reads like a sophisticated weekend broadsheet with time-stamped activity blocks and RSVP avatar clusters.

  • Warm daylight paper aesthetic
  • Specific time slots (10 AM, 7 PM)
  • Illustrated category art banners
  • Zero blurry background halos
Option C · Dub.co & Linear

The Dub.co & Amie Precision Dashboard

Minimalist dark-slate dashboard with 1px subtle borders, prominent Thursday Lock countdown timer, live vote progress meters (8 / 5 votes needed to win), and real-time circle presence feed.

  • Thursday Lock Countdown header
  • Vote progress milestone bars
  • Active circle member presence rail
  • Quiet chrome & crisp 1px borders
